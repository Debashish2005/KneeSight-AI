# Result Guidance Safety

The result page provides navigation guidance, not diagnosis, severity grading,
treatment, or prescriptions.

## Interpretation Rules

- The abnormal-class percentage is model output, not disease severity.
- A Normal prediction is not an all-clear result.
- A prediction near the configured decision threshold is presented as
  uncertain.
- Persistent or worsening symptoms require professional review regardless of
  the model output.
- Medication must not be started, stopped, or changed based on this app.

## Suggested Care Navigation

- Obtain the official radiology interpretation of the original MRI.
- Start with the referring clinician, primary clinician, or radiologist.
- Orthopedics or sports medicine may be appropriate for injury, instability,
  pain, swelling, or limited movement.
- Seek prompt medical assessment for severe pain after injury, inability to
  bear weight, deformity, numbness, or a hot swollen joint with fever.
